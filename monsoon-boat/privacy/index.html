Privacy Policy — Monsoon Boat

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Monsoon Boat ("the game", "we") collects and how it is used. Contact: abmishra700@gmail.com

What we collect

DataWhyShared publicly?

We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or microphone. Please do not use your real name as your player name.

Where the data is stored

Your game is saved on your phone. Your own levels are also made on your phone. Backups and the leaderboard use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Firebase may process technical data such as IP address and device information to provide the service. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising and purchases

  • Ads: the game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional "watch an ad" rewards, and sometimes an ad between levels. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and IP address to show, limit and measure ads. In the EU/UK you are asked for consent first; on iPhone, ads are only personalised if you allow tracking. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your phone settings. Only family-friendly (rated G) ads are requested. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Purchases: the game sells coin packs and "No Ads". Payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store / Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only learn which product was bought, to deliver it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game reminds you about full lives, your daily gift and new levels. These reminders are planned on your phone — nothing is sent to us. Switch them off any time: Profile → ALERTS.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while you play. Delete it yourself, inside the game: Profile → Delete account. This deletes your leaderboard entry, your backup code, your online player ID and all progress on the phone, straight away. You can also email abmishra700@gmail.com with your player name and we will delete it for you. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device (a backup code stays until you delete your account).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy in the game's store listing.
