Monsoon Boat · data deletion

Delete it yourself, inside the game (instant)

  1. Open Monsoon Boat.
  2. Tap your avatar (top left) to open your Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom, then DELETE.

This deletes, straight away: your leaderboard entry (name, avatar, stars), your backup code and the saved progress inside it, your online player ID, and all progress on the phone. Nothing is kept.

Or ask us by email

Can't open the game any more? Email abmishra700@gmail.com with your player name (and your backup code if you have one). We delete the leaderboard entry and backup within 30 days and reply when done.

Purchases are handled by Google Play / the App Store and are not part of your game account. A purchased "No Ads" can be restored with "Restore purchases" in the game's Shop.
